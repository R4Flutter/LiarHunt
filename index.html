            How to play

            The police checked some facts. They're true. Then three suspects tell you their story.

            One story can't be true. Compare each one with the facts and accuse the liar.

            Then prove it. Tap the fact the story breaks. Some lies only break when you put two facts together.

            Twist cases

            Every 5th case changes the rules. Sometimes two people are lying and you must find the honest one. Sometimes everyone is honest and one fact is fake: then the proof is the story that shows it.

            Stars

            • No mistakes, no Magnifier
            • One mistake, or a Magnifier
            • Two mistakes

            A third mistake makes the case fall apart: you retry that step, and the mission can earn 1 star.

            Modes

            Daily Case: 5 cases a day, the same for everyone, one try each. A wrong suspect or a wrong proof lets the liar walk. Career: Book 1, The Black Ledger, one story in 30 missions. You search scenes, question suspects, rebuild timelines, compare documents and connect the evidence. Rush: a quick game against the clock: catch as many liars as you can before you run out of time or lives.

            Daily 5

            Most days the Daily is one mystery in five connected cases. Each case opens the next, and what you prove goes into your Notebook for the cases ahead. One wrong answer ends the day, so take your time. You get one free Hunch a day, and the full story opens at midnight (India time).

            Streaks, shields and keys

            Play the Daily every day to grow your streak. A Streak Shield saves it if you miss a day. A Case Key opens a Daily you missed, with full rewards. You earn one of each every 7 days in a row.

            Keyboard: keys 1-5 pick, Enter confirms, Esc cancels.
