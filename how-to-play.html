How to play

Every case has a few facts the police checked and three suspects telling their story. One story can't be true. Find it.

The rules

  1. Read the facts. They are always true. The police checked them.
  2. Read each suspect's story. Two of them fit the facts. One of them doesn't.
  3. Accuse the liar. Pick the suspect whose story breaks a fact, then confirm.
  4. Prove it. Tap the fact the story breaks. Some lies only break when you put two facts together, and the game tells you how many facts to pick.

A guess isn't enough: the right suspect with the wrong proof lets the liar walk. After every case the game shows the proof, so even a miss teaches you something.

An example case

The Missing Cupcakes. A box of cupcakes vanished from the school kitchen. The police checked three facts:

  • The kitchen door was locked at 3 PM.
  • It rained hard all afternoon.
  • The cupcakes were last seen at 2 PM.

Rosa the cook says she locked the kitchen at three and went home with her umbrella. Leo the teacher says he was marking tests in his classroom until four. Mia the student says she played football outside all afternoon and the sun was shining.

The liar: Mia. The proof: "It rained hard all afternoon", so there was no sunshine for her football game. Rosa's story fits the locked door and the rain, and Leo's story doesn't break any fact.

Ways to play

  • Daily Case: 5 new cases every day, the same for every player. Keep your streak going and share your result.
  • Career: Book 1, The Black Ledger: one mystery in 30 missions. A financial analyst has vanished, and the trail leads to a bribery network and a mole inside your own agency. You search scenes, question suspects and catch their lies with evidence, rebuild timelines, compare documents and connect the clues, then accuse the culprit with proof. A mistake or a hint costs a star; you can always try again.
  • Rush: catch as many liars as you can before the clock runs out. Every catch adds time, and you have 3 lives. Every 5 cases a new stage starts with a new rule, like a faster clock or facts hidden in fog.
  • Play live: open a room and send one link. Up to 10 friends play the same 5 cases at the same time, with a live scoreboard. A proven catch scores 100 points, plus up to 50 for speed.
  • Write a case: make up your own mystery, hide one lie in it and send it to friends as a link. They get one try to catch the liar and prove it.

Twist cases

Some cases flip the rules, and the case tells you when:

  • Two are lying: find the only suspect who tells the truth, then prove the other two lied.
  • Everyone is honest: all three stories are true, so one of the "facts" must be fake. Find it, then tap the story that proves it.

Tips for catching liars

  • Take notes while you read. Tap a fact to tick it off, and tap the pencil on a suspect to mark them as "checks out" or "suspicious". Notes never count as an answer.
  • Check every story against every fact, one at a time. The lie always clashes with the facts, never with someone's character. Sometimes it takes two facts together: a closing time and a walking time, a day of the week and a date.
  • Watch for times, weather, places and who had a key. Those are the details liars get wrong.
  • A story that sounds odd isn't automatically a lie. Only a broken fact counts.
  • Stuck? The Magnifier hint points you in the right direction, but it costs your third star.

Questions

Is LiarHunt free?

Yes. It runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. There's nothing to download and no account to create.

When do new cases come out?

A new set of 5 Daily cases arrives every day at midnight.

I missed a day. Can I still play it?

You can read every past Daily and its answers on the past cases page. A missed day stays missed, but a Streak Shield saves your streak.

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is saved online in an account the game makes for you. Sign in with Google in your profile and it follows you to any phone or browser.

Can I play with friends?

Add up to 8 friends with your friend code and see their league and Daily result. Share your Daily result and it becomes a challenge, or play live with friends in a room. Every season you climb the league from Bronze to Diamond.
