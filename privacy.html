Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 (progress now saved to your account, with optional Google sign-in)

The short version

Your progress is saved online, in an account the game makes for you on your first visit. That account is anonymous: no email, password or name. If you choose to sign in with Google, your progress is linked to your Google account so it follows you to any device. Your Daily result is counted on the leaderboard without a name, so players can see how many people played. Your name is only shown to others if you pick one.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress isn't kept on your device: it's saved to your account (see "Your progress and your account" below). Your browser keeps the sign-in for that account, so the game knows it's you next time. Clearing your browser data signs you out: if you signed in with Google, sign in again and your progress comes back.

Your browser may also keep a copy of the game's files so it opens faster. Nothing about you is in those files.

Advertising

This site may show ads from Google AdSense to keep the game free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure them, based on your visits to this and other websites. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland), you'll be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible) to count visits and see which parts of the game people use, such as how many cases are finished. It doesn't use cookies, and the data is grouped and isn't used to identify you.

Your progress and your account

The game saves your progress on Google Firebase: today's answers, your level, coins, stars and streaks, your shields and keys, your stats, the groups you joined and your settings. It's saved under a random, anonymous account ID that the game creates on your first visit, and only that account can read it. It's updated while you play and at the end of every case.

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, Firebase links your Google account to the same account ID and keeps your Google email address and name with it, so you can sign in on another device and carry on. We don't post anything to your Google account and don't see your password. You can sign out from your profile at any time.

Before accounts, the game backed up progress under a 12-letter save code. Those backups stay on Firebase so an old code can still bring progress into your account. No new codes are made.

Deleting your data: "Reset all progress" in Settings deletes your saved progress. To also have your account and its Google link deleted, contact us (see Contact).

The leaderboard

When you finish the Daily Case, your result (how many liars you caught, your time and the colored grid) is posted to the day's leaderboard under the same anonymous ID, without a name. It's shown as an "unnamed detective" and is used to count players and work out percentiles, like "better than 64% of players".

Joining the leaderboard is optional. If you join, the game stores the detective name you choose, linked to the same anonymous ID, with your daily scores, your weekly league points, your season score and your best endless run. Your name and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard, in the weekly league and on the season scoreboard. This data is kept on Google Firebase. Pick a name that doesn't identify you if you'd rather stay private.

Live rooms

When you open or join a live room, your detective name, your answers and your score for that room are stored on Google Firebase under the same anonymous ID. Anyone with the room's link or code can see the room's players and scores. Rooms aren't listed anywhere, and you can leave a room at any time.

Cases you write

When you write a case for your friends, the finished case is packed into the link you send: it isn't stored on our servers, and only people who have the link can open it. While you're still writing it, it's saved with your progress so you can come back to it.

Friend groups

If you create or join a friend group, the group's name and the detective names of its members are stored on Google Firebase. Anyone with the group's code can see the group and its members' daily scores. You can leave a group at any time.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share, the game creates a short text with your score and a challenge link. The link holds your detective name, your result and your time, so a friend can try to beat you. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to paste or send it.

Daily reminder

The daily reminder is a normal event in your own calendar (Google Calendar or a calendar file). The game doesn't send notifications and doesn't see your calendar.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy or the game? Message us on Instagram or the game's GitHub page.
